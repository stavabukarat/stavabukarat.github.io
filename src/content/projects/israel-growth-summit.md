## The problem

The Israel Growth Forum needed a site to introduce its annual summit and take RSVPs, and those RSVPs had to reach the team's management system without anyone copying them by hand.

## What I built

A Wix Studio website for the summit:

- **Event pages** introducing the summit, its speakers, agenda and the forum behind it.
- **RSVP with Wix Events**, so the event and its registrations are managed in one place.
- **Webhook integration** that sends every RSVP to the [summit management system](/projects/israel-growth-summit-management/) I built in Base44, where the team approves attendees and handles emails and check-in.

## Stack

Wix Studio, Wix Events, webhooks.
