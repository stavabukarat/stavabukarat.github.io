## The problem

After October 7th, many memorial projects were created: memorial sites, testimonies, data projects, media and research. They were scattered across the web, with no single place to find them.

## What I built

"Lizkor Yachad" (Remember Together), a hub that gathers these initiatives into one browsable portfolio:

- **Project directory** where visitors can browse the memorial initiatives in one place.
- **Public submission form** so anyone can suggest a project to add.
- **Manager dashboard** where the team reviews submissions and approves or rejects them before they go live.
- **Personal memorial pages** that families can set up for free to share memories of their loved ones.

## Stack

Base44, AI-assisted development.
