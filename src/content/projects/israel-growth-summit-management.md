## The problem

The Israel Growth Summit is the annual event of the Israel Growth Forum. Managing attendees was disorganized: RSVPs, approvals, emails and check-in were all handled by hand, in different places.

## What I built

A management system in Base44 connected to the summit's Wix Studio website:

- **RSVP sync**: every RSVP made on the Wix site is sent by webhook into the management system, so all attendees are collected in one place.
- **Approval workflow**: the team reviews each attendee and approves or rejects them from the dashboard.
- **Automated emails**: approved and rejected attendees get an email automatically, with the event details for those approved.
- **Reminder automations** sent to approved attendees before the event.
- **Check-in** at the entrance on the day of the summit.

This is a private internal tool, so there's no public link.

## Stack

Base44, Wix Events, webhooks, email automations.
