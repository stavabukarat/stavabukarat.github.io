## The problem

Shahar's family and friends wanted a way to tell his story: the battles he fought on October 7th and in the war that followed, up to his death in combat in Gaza in 2025. They wanted people to walk his path step by step, not just read about it.

## What I built

An interactive, station-by-station journey built on Base44:

- **Animated map** that moves along Shahar's route as the visitor advances from one station to the next.
- **Rich media at every station**: photos, testimonies, and audio clips from a podcast in which Shahar tells, in his own voice, what he saw and felt.
- **Paced storytelling**: each station opens only when the visitor chooses to continue, so the story unfolds one step at a time.
- Full Hebrew (RTL) experience.

## Stack

Base44, AI-assisted development.
