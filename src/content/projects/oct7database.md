## The problem

Prof. Yuval Harpaz maintains a comprehensive database documenting those killed and taken hostage in the war that began on October 7th, 2023, across all fronts. The data needed to be presented in a way that is clear, respectful and easy to explore.

## What I built

A Wix Studio website built around the database:

- **Custom filters written in Velo code**, letting visitors search and narrow the database by the fields that matter to them.
- **Visual data presentation**, including maps and data views that make the numbers readable.
- **Full English and Hebrew support**, including right-to-left layouts for Hebrew.

## Stack

Wix Studio, Velo (JavaScript), Wix CMS.
